# Troubleshooting

## No selection controls appear

Confirm the extension is enabled, refresh the page, and enable selection from the popup. Chrome internal pages are restricted. Custom and virtualized page layouts may not be supported.

## An action is missing

The control may not expose a clear accessible name, tooltip, icon name, or menu pattern. BulkOperation does not guess based only on position.

## A batch reports failures

The page may have changed, navigated, opened an unsupported confirmation dialog, or removed a control. Review the result list before retrying.

Report reproducible issues at https://github.com/sourabhJainR/BulkOperation/issues. Remove private data first.
